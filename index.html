Privacy Policy for PDF Flipbook

Last updated: 2026-11-02

PDF Flipbook ("the app") is a PDF reader that shows documents like a book. This policy explains what information the app handles. Contact: devinnosoftnet@gmail.com

Your PDF files stay on your device

  • The app opens PDF files you choose with your device's file picker. Files are read and rendered locally on your device.
  • Your PDFs, their text, your search queries, your library (bookshelf), reading positions and thumbnails are stored only on your device. We do not upload, copy, view or have access to them.
  • The app has no account system, no sign-in, and no servers of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (banner ads and occasional full-screen ads between reading sessions; never over a page you are reading).

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use, from your device:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version) and ad interaction/diagnostic data

We do not receive this data and do not combine it with anything else. How Google handles it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Where required (for example the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Permissions

The app does not request special permissions. It needs internet access only to load ads.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your device; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. We hold no data on our side to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.
